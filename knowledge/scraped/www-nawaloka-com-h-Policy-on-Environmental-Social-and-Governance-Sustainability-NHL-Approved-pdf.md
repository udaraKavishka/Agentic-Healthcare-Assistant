Policy on Environmental, Social and Governance Sustainability – NHL 1 
 
POLICY ON ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) SUSTAINABILITY 
NAWALOKA HOSPITALS PLC 
 
This policy is drafted as per the Colombo Stock Exchange Corporate Governance rule 9.2.1 (h). 
 
1. DEFINITIONS 
 ‘Board’: Board of Directors of Nawaloka Hospitals PLC 
 ‘Company’: Nawaloka Hospitals PLC 
 ‘CSE’: Colombo Stock Exchange 
 ‘ESG’: Environmental, Social and Governance 
 ‘Listing Rules’: Listing Rules of the Colombo Stock Exchange 
 
2. PURPOSE AND SCOPE 
 
Promoting Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) sustainability is a strategic priority for 
Nawaloka Hospitals PLC. The ESG Policy aims to ensure regulatory compliance, satisfy stakeholder 
expectations and contribute positively to society. As part of the Nawaloka Group, the Company’s ESG 
Policy aligns with the broader framework established by the Group, while ensuring compliance with 
local laws and regulations in Sri Lanka. 
 
This policy addresses environmental management, social responsibility and gover nance processes 
across all Company activities. Any new business ventures or significant investments will adhere to this 
ESG Policy. 
 
3. GOVERNING PRINCIPLES OF THE ESG POLICY 
 
3.1 Accountability 
The Board and the Nominations and Governance Committee are responsible for overseeing the 
Company’s ESG performance. 
 
3.2 Transparency 
The Company commits to transparent reporting and open dialogue with stakeholders regarding its 
ESG policies and performance. 
 
3.3 Sustainability 
The ESG Policy focus es on long -term sustainability, reducing environmental impact, and improving 
resource efficiency. 
 
3.4 Performance Measurement 
The Company will establish measurable ESG goals and track progress against these targets. 
 
4. ESG ROADMAP 
 
The Company’s ESG performance will be assessed against internal Company targets. Progress on each 
ESG initiative will be reported in the Company’s Annual Report for transparency with stakeholders. 
 
5. COMPONENTS OF THE ESG POLICY 
 
5.1 Environmental Factors 
The Company will promote sustainable practices, focusing on reducing environmental impact and 
implementing the Company’s resources efficiency program.

5.2 Social Factors 
The Company adheres to global standards for human rights, employee welfare and labo ur practices. 
Key initiatives include: 
 Respect for human rights, as outlined in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights 
 Health and safety, diversity and inclusion programs 
 Zero tolerance for forced or child labour 
 
5.3 Governance Factors 
The Company upholds the highest governance standards, including: 
 Anti-Bribery and Corruption policies 
 Ethical business practices 
 Transparent operations with ongoing training on ethics and integrity 
 Strong monitoring systems to ensure compliance with all relevant laws, regulations 
 
6. COMPLIANCE AND DISCLOSURE 
 
6.1 Regulatory Compliance 
The Company will fully comply with the CSE Listing Rules and any other applicable regulations 
concerning ESG. 
 
6.2 Disclosure 
The Company will disclose its ESG compliance in its Annual Report, ensuring transparency in alignment 
with the CSE Listing Rules.
