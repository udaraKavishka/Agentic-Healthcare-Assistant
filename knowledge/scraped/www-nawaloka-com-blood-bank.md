# Nawaloka Hospital Colombo Blood Bank
Lifesaving Blood Donation & Transfusion Services in Sri Lanka

Welcome to the Nawaloka Hospitals Blood Bank, one of the most trusted and advanced blood donation and transfusion centers in Sri Lanka. As a leading healthcare provider in the region, we are committed to ensuring the availability of safe, high-quality blood and blood components for patients in need. Our blood bank plays a vital role in supporting surgeries, trauma treatment, cancer treatment, maternity services, and chronic illness management through timely access to life-saving blood products.
Why a Reliable Blood Bank Matters
A well-equipped and efficiently managed blood bank is the backbone of any modern healthcare facility. At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo, our Blood Bank Services ensure 24/7 access to safe and screened blood. Whether it’s an emergency surgery, an accident, or long-term treatment like chemotherapy, the availability of blood components-including whole blood, red cells, plasma, and platelets-can mean the difference between life and death.
We follow globally recognized standards for blood collection, processing, storage, and cross-matching, ensuring safety and compatibility for all recipients.
Blood Donation in Sri Lanka - Save Lives in Just 15 Minutes
Blood donation in Sri Lanka is a voluntary and noble act that saves thousands of lives each year. At Nawaloka Hospitals, we encourage healthy individuals to become regular donors. A single unit of blood can help save up to three lives, especially when it is separated into its components-red cells, plasma, and platelets.
Our state-of-the-art blood bank in Colombo welcomes voluntary donors, both walk-ins and organized group drives. Every donation is screened for infections, including HIV, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, Syphilis, and Malaria, ensuring the safety of recipients and peace of mind for donors.
