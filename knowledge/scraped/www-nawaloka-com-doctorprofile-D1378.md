## Profile

Professional background and areas of expertise

Dr. Harindu Wijesinghe is a highly respected consultant in rheumatology and sports medicine, renowned for his expertise in managing musculoskeletal, rheumatological, sports-related injuries, and autoimmune conditions.

At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo - Sri Lanka’s No. 1 private hospital, Dr. Wijesinghe provides world-class, patient-centered care, with a strong focus on restoring health, mobility, and overall quality of life.

In addition to his clinical expertise, Dr. Wijesinghe incorporates advanced patient information systems and AI-assisted data analysis to enhance diagnosis, personalize treatment, and deliver safer, more effective care.

Areas of Expertise
• Management of arthritis and autoimmune diseases
• Diagnosis and Treatment of joint and muscle pain
• Sports injury management and preventive care
• Preventive musculoskeletal health strategies

Services at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo
• Comprehensive arthritis and autoimmune disease clinics
• Advanced diagnostic imaging for musculoskeletal conditions
• Sports medicine and injury-prevention clinics
• 24/7 emergency care for acute musculoskeletal conditions
• Collaborative, multidisciplinary care for complex cases
