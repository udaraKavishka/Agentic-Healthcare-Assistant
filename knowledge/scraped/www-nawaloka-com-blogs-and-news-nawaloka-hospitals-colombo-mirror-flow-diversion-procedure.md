## Nawaloka Hospitals PLC Colombo Sets a National Milestone with Mirror Flow Diversion Procedure

Nawaloka Hospitals PLC Colombo has marked a significant milestone in Sri Lanka’s health services landscape by successfully performing a Mirror Flow Diversion procedure for Bilateral Internal Carotid Artery (ICA) Aneurysms. This groundbreaking achievement underscores the hospital’s commitment to bringing world-class medical innovations to Sri Lanka and further strengthens its position as a leader in advanced neurovascular expertise. 
Brain aneurysms occur when a weakened section of a blood vessel wall bulges outward, creating a potentially life-threatening condition. If an aneurysm ruptures, it can result in severe brain hemorrhage, stroke, permanent neurological damage, or even death. Bilateral ICA aneurysms, where aneurysms develop in both internal carotid arteries supplying blood to the brain, are particularly complex and require highly specialized treatment. 
Nawaloka Hospitals PLC Colombo, successfully completed the above procedure using a specialized flow diverter stent, an advanced endovascular device designed to treat complex aneurysms by redirecting blood flow away from the aneurysm, promoting safe and effective healing. 
One of the most significant advantages of this procedure is that it eliminates the need for conventional open brain surgery. Performed through a small catheter inserted via the wrist or groin, the treatment is less invasive, resulting in reduced surgical risks, minimal scarring, shorter hospital stays, and faster recovery times. Patients can often resume their daily activities much sooner compared to traditional surgical interventions. 
The introduction of this advanced procedure in Sri Lanka is a testament to Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo’s continuous investment in cutting-edge technology, specialist expertise, and patient-oriented services. Supported by state-of-the-art imaging facilities and a highly skilled multidisciplinary team of neuro-interventional specialists, the hospital successfully delivered a treatment that demonstrates its capability in managing highly complex neurovascular conditions. 
The successful execution of the Mirror Flow Diversion procedure for Bilateral ICA Aneurysms also highlights the growing sophistication of the nation’s health services sector. It demonstrates that patients can now receive advanced neurovascular treatments comparable to those offered at leading international medical centers, right here in Sri Lanka. 
Early diagnosis remains essential for successful outcomes. Individuals experiencing persistent headaches, vision disturbances, neurological symptoms, or those with a family history of aneurysms should seek prompt medical consultation. Timely intervention can significantly reduce the risk of serious complications and improve long-term health outcomes. 
This achievement reflects Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo’s ongoing mission to push the boundaries of medical excellence and bring the latest advancements in health services to Sri Lanka. As the hospital pioneers in innovative treatment options, it remains dedicated to improving patient outcomes and shaping the future of health services in the country.
