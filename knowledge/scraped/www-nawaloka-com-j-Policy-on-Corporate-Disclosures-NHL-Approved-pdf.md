POLICY FOR CORPORATE DISCLOSURES 
NAWALOKA HOSPITALS PLC 
 
This policy is drafted as per the Colombo Stock Exchange Corporate Governance rule 9.2.1 (j). 
1. Introduction 
This Corporate Disclosure Policy outlines the key information that Nawaloka Hospitals PLC is required to 
disclose to the public and specifies the communication channels that will be utilized for such disclosures. 
The aim is to ensure transparency, accuracy and consistency in all disclosures made to all stakeholders. 
2. Disclosure and Transparency 
All information disclosed to all stakeholders must be clear, concise and accurate. The information should 
not mislead the audience and should focus on provid ing relevant details about the company’s products 
and services. English will be the primary language for communication, and where necessary, translations 
in Sinhala and/or Tamil will be provided. 
2.1. Communications 
All mass communications to patients and customers, whether corporate or product-related, shall contain 
essential details about the relevant product, servi ce or announcement. Additional information will be 
available on the company’s official website. 
The company will also use patient information provi ded to communicate important updates, notices, 
promotional details and other information relevant to the patients. 
2.2. Communication Channels 
The mode of communication used for corporate disclosures will depend on the nature of the information. 
The company’s official website will serve as the pr imary channel for disseminating key information 
regarding services and products. The company may al so use mass media, social media and other digital 
platforms (e.g., advertisements) to reach patients and the general public. 
Additional channels may include: 
 E-notifications 
 SMS 
 Statements 
 Letters 
 Other relevant methods, including personal contact details, to ensure patients are informed.

2.3. Information Sources 
The company’s website will provide detailed information about its products and services.
