## What Is IUI Treatment?

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI) is a minimally invasive fertility treatment where specially prepared sperm is placed directly into the uterus during ovulation to increase the chances of conception. 

### IUI Treatment Is Commonly Recommended For:

Unexplained infertility 

Mild male factor infertility 

Ovulation-related challenges 

Couples trying to conceive naturally without success 
Our fertility specialists evaluate your condition thoroughly to confirm whether IUI treatment is the right option for you.
