## Profile

Professional background and areas of expertise

Dr. Riaz Mowjood is a highly respected consultant chest specialist, renowned for his expertise in respiratory medicine and dedication to patient health. At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo, Sri Lanka’s No. 1 private hospital, Dr. Mowjood provides world-class care for respiratory conditions with a focus on compassion and precision.
His patient-first approach makes him a trusted choice for those seeking expert respiratory care.

Expertise in Respiratory Care You Can Trust
Dr. Riaz Mowjood specializes in:
• Management of asthma and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)
• Diagnosis and treatment of lung infections
• Treatment of interstitial lung diseases
• Sleep apnea and respiratory sleep disorders
• Pulmonary function testing and bronchoscopy
• Preventive respiratory care and counseling
His thorough approach ensures personalized treatment plans for optimal lung health.

Respiratory Services at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo
Nawaloka Hospitals is a leader in respiratory care, offering advanced services under specialists like Dr. Mowjood. Key services include:
• Advanced pulmonary function testing
• Bronchoscopy and lung biopsy
• Comprehensive asthma and COPD clinics
• Sleep study and sleep apnea management
• 24/7 emergency respiratory care
• Clinics on Interstitial lung disease
• Multidisciplinary support for complex cases
Nawaloka’s state of the art facilities ensure accurate diagnostics and effective treatments.
