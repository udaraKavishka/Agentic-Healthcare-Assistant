## Profile

Professional background and areas of expertise

Dr Sandeep Sharma is a seasoned and highly accomplished Consultant Cardiac Anaesthetist and Intensivist with over 25 years of experience in anaesthesia and more than two decades of dedicated practice in cardiac anaesthesia. Trained under the esteemed Mumbai University, with foundational experience at the renowned Tata Memorial Hospital, bringing robust expertise in onco-anaesthesia and critical care alongside a strong cardiac focus.
Currently serving in a dual role as Consultant Cardiac Anaesthetist and Intensivist, providing comprehensive perioperative care for high-risk patients undergoing complex cardiac, renal transplant, vascular, and neurosurgical procedures. Skilled in managing advanced haemodynamic monitoring, transesophageal echocardiography (TEE), cardiopulmonary bypass, and intensive care management.
Known for a calm, precise, and evidence-based approach in high-pressure environments, with a reputation for excellence in multidisciplinary collaboration and patient-centred care.
