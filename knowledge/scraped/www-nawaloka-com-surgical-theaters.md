# Sri Lanka’s Best Surgical Theatres
Where Surgical Precision Meets Compassionate Treatment
At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo 02, our surgical theatres are a cornerstone of our commitment to clinical excellence. Recognized among the best surgical theatres in Colombo, we provide a safe, technologically advanced environment where lives are transformed every day through expert surgical treatment.

Whether you're preparing for a planned procedure or facing an emergency, you can trust that your surgery will be performed in one of Sri Lanka’s most advanced operating theatres, supported by highly skilled teams who prioritize your safety and wellbeing.

A World-Class Surgical Environment
Our modern surgical complex is equipped with cutting-edge technology, precision monitoring systems, and state-of-the-art sterilization protocols that meet global standards. Each operating room is designed to reduce infection risk, support complex procedures, and provide surgeons with the tools they need for optimal outcomes.
From minimally invasive laparoscopic surgeries to high-precision neurosurgical and cardiac procedures, Nawaloka’s surgical theatres are ready for it all - 24/7.
Expert Teams. Trusted Outcomes.
Every procedure at Nawaloka is carried out by some of the most trusted surgical experts in Colombo, supported by experienced anesthesiologists, surgical nurses, and post-operative treatment staff. Our multi-disciplinary teams work together to ensure seamless, coordinated service at every stage of your surgical journey.
