## Areas of Specialisations

Clinical expertise and specialist cardiac anaesthesia services

Dr. Waikkar is a highly skilled consultant cardiac anaesthetist, renowned for his expertise in ensuring patient safety during complex cardiac procedures. At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo, Sri Lanka’s premier private hospital, Dr. Waikkar delivers world-class anaesthetic care with a focus on precision and patient comfort.
His dedication to excellence makes him a trusted choice for cardiac surgeries and interventions.

Expertise in Cardiac Anaesthesia You Can Trust
Dr. Waikkar specializes in:

Anaesthesia for coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG)

Anaesthetic management for valve replacement surgeries

Support for minimally invasive cardiac procedures

Pain management for post-operative cardiac patients

Critical care anaesthesia for high-risk cases

Pre-operative assessments and optimization
His meticulous approach ensures safe and comfortable experiences for patients undergoing cardiac procedures.

## Cardiac Anaesthesia Services at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo

Nawaloka Hospitals is a leader in cardiac care, offering advanced anaesthetic services under specialists like Dr. Waikkar. Key services include:

Advanced anaesthesia for open-heart surgeries

Anaesthetic support for angiograms and angioplasties

Comprehensive pre- and post-operative care

Critical care monitoring in cardiac ICU

24/7 emergency cardiac anaesthesia services

Multidisciplinary support for complex cases
Nawaloka’s modern facilities ensure the highest standards of safety and care.
