Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

###
1. When should I consult a neurologist in Colombo?

You should consult a neurologist Colombo if you experience persistent headaches, seizures, dizziness, memory loss, numbness, muscle weakness, balance problems, tremors, or sudden neurological symptoms such as difficulty speaking or weakness on one side of the body.

###
2. What neurological conditions are treated at Nawaloka Neuro Centre?

Our specialists diagnose and treat stroke, epilepsy, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, migraines, dementia, peripheral neuropathy, brain tumors, spinal disorders, movement disorders, and many other neurological conditions.

###
3. When should I see a neurosurgeon in Colombo?

A neurosurgeon Colombo may be required for conditions such as brain tumors, spinal disorders, traumatic brain injuries, nerve compression, hydrocephalus, or neurological conditions that require surgical treatment after specialist evaluation.

###
4. What neurological investigations are available?

Our Neuro Centre offers MRI, CT scans, EEG, EMG, nerve conduction studies, neurovascular imaging, and comprehensive neurological assessments to accurately diagnose neurological disorders.

###
5. What treatments are available for stroke patients?

We provide emergency stroke assessment, thrombolysis where appropriate, neurological monitoring, rehabilitation services, medication management, and long-term follow-up care to support recovery.

###
6. Do you provide rehabilitation after neurological treatment?

Yes. Our neurorehabilitation program supports patients recovering from stroke, brain injury, spinal cord injury, neurological surgery, and other neurological conditions through individualized rehabilitation plans.

###
7. Are minimally invasive neurosurgical procedures available?

Yes. Our experienced neurosurgeon Colombo perform selected minimally invasive brain and spine procedures whenever clinically appropriate to support faster recovery and reduce surgical risks.

###
8. How do I book an appointment with a neurologist?

You can directly schedule an appointment with one of our experienced neurologists for consultation, neurological investigations, treatment planning, or follow-up care.

###
9. What should I bring to my neurology consultation?

Bring previous medical records, imaging reports, laboratory results, medication lists, referral letters (if available), and details about your symptoms to help our specialists perform a comprehensive assessment.

###
10. Why choose Nawaloka Neuro Centre?

Nawaloka Neuro Centre combines experienced neurologists in Colombo, skilled neurosurgeons in Colombo, advanced diagnostic technology, modern neurosurgical facilities, and comprehensive rehabilitation services to provide high-quality neurological care tailored to every patient's needs.
