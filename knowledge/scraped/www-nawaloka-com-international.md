Services for International Patients

## Every detail handled with precision.

Our International Patients Team is dedicated to delivering world-class service, ensuring a hassle-free medical journey for both you and your family.

##

Pre-Arrival Medical Opinion

Our team of doctors will provide you with a detailed medical opinion before your arrival. Please send us your medical details and history, and we will arrange an appointment with the appropriate specialist. Note that for accuracy, we may seek additional information based on the nature of your inquiry.

##

Visa Services

We offer assistance with visa services to streamline your travel preparations.

##

Airport Pickup

We provide airport pickup services for your convenience, ensuring a smooth admission process.

##

Foreign Currency Exchange

We offer foreign currency exchange facilities to assist you during your stay.

##

Dedicated Coordinators

Each patient is assigned a dedicated coordinator who will take care of your needs within the hospital and assist you with administrative processes.

##

Payment Options

Payments can be made using cash or credit cards, providing flexibility for your financial preferences.

##

Accommodation Assistance

If you prefer to stay at a nearby hotel during your treatment, our International Marketing Team can help you find accommodation that aligns with your budget. Please contact our International Representative before your travel period for more details.

##

Interpreters

We have interpreters available at the hospital to ensure effective communication during your stay.
