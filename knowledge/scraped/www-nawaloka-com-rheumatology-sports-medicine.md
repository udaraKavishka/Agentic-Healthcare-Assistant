# Expert Rheumatology & Sports Medicine in Colombo - Nawaloka Hospitals Sri Lanka
Move Freely. Live Fully. Get Relief with Trusted Rheumatology & Sports Medicine Experts.
At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo 02, we specialize in restoring movement, relieving pain, and improving quality of life through our advanced Rheumatology & Sports Medicine services. Whether you’re living with arthritis, recovering from a sports injury, or managing an autoimmune condition, our experienced team is here to help with compassion and clinical precision. Why Choose Us?
As one of the best rheumatology treatment centers in Sri Lanka, our department brings together board-certified rheumatologists, orthopedic surgeons, and physiotherapists to deliver comprehensive and personalized treatment. We go beyond symptom relief we focus on long-term healing, joint preservation, and empowering you to move without limits. Conditions We Treat
Our expert team provides accurate diagnosis and effective treatment for a wide range of musculoskeletal and autoimmune conditions, including:
Rheumatoid arthritis & osteoarthritis
Ankylosing spondylitis
Gout & lupus (SLE)
Tendonitis & bursitis
Osteoporosis
Joint and muscle inflammation
Sports-related injuries sprains, ligament tears, stress fractures, overuse injuries Services We Offer

✅ Joint & soft tissue ultrasound diagnostics
✅ Minimally invasive pain relief procedures
✅ Personalized physiotherapy and rehabilitation plans
✅ Medication management for autoimmune diseases
✅ Sports injury prevention and performance programs
✅ Fracture recovery, bracing, and post-operative rehab
✅ Collaborative service between specialists for optimal recovery
