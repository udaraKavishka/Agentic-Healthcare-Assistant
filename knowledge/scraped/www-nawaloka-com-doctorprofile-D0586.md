## Profile

Professional background and areas of expertise 

Dr. Duminda Pathirane is a highly regarded paediatrician, dedicated to providing exceptional care for children from infancy through adolescence. At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo, Sri Lanka’s No. 1 private hospital, Dr. Pathirane delivers world-class care with a focus on compassion and expertise.
His child-friendly approach makes him a trusted choice for families across Sri Lanka. 

Expertise in Paediatric Care You Can Trust
Dr. Duminda Pathirane specializes in: 
• Well-child check-ups and vaccinations 
• Management of childhood infections 
• Growth and developmental assessments 
• Nutritional counseling for children 
• Treatment of acute and chronic paediatric conditions 
• Parental education and support
His warm demeanor ensures children and families feel at ease during every visit.

Paediatric Services at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo
Nawaloka Hospitals is a leader in paediatric care, offering advanced services under specialists like Dr. Pathirane. Key services include: 
• Comprehensive well-child programs 
• Advanced diagnostic imaging for children 
• Vaccination and immunization clinics 
• Multidisciplinary care for complex conditions 
• 24/7 paediatric emergency services 
• Child-friendly facilities for comfort

Nawaloka’s modern infrastructure ensures the highest standards of care for young patients.
