## Advanced Medical Imaging Technologies

Our Radiology Unit features state-of-the-art diagnostic tools, optimized with AI algorithms: 

Digital X-rays Provide rapid and accurate imaging for diagnosing various conditions, with reduced radiation exposure. 

AI-Powered CT Scanners Generate detailed cross-sectional images of the body, enhanced by AI for improved detection and evaluation of diseases. 

AI-Enhanced MRI Scanners Utilize powerful magnetic fields and radio waves to produce comprehensive images of internal structures, with AI improving image analysis for conditions such as tumors and neurological disorders. 

Ultrasound Technology Create real-time images using sound waves, helping to diagnose conditions affecting the abdomen, pelvis, and vascular system. 

Interventional Radiology Offers targeted therapies through procedures such as angiography and biopsies, minimizing the need for invasive surgery. 

3D Mammography Employs advanced imaging to provide a three-dimensional view of breast tissue, improving detection accuracy and visualization of abnormalities. 

DEXA Scans Measures bone density to assess osteoporosis risk, providing critical information for monitoring bone health. 

CT Angiography Non-invasive imaging technique that visualizes blood vessels, aiding in the diagnosis of conditions such as arterial blockages or aneurysms.
