## Profile

Professional background and areas of expertise

Dr. Punsith is a highly experienced brain, spine, and nerve surgeon in Sri Lanka,
with over 19 years of experience treating both simple and complex neurological
conditions.

He has worked in leading Government hospitals in Anuradhapura, Badulla, and
was a key figure in setting up the Neurosurgery Unit at Kurunegala Teaching
Hospital.

Since 2015, Dr. Punsith has been practicing at Nawaloka Hospital Colombo,
offering advanced treatment for brain tumors, spine problems, nerve disorders,
stroke, and injuries. He treats both adults and children.

Dr. Punsith also shares his knowledge as an Honorary Senior Lecturer in
Neuroanatomy at the University of Kelaniya.

Conditions Treated – Brain, Spine & Nerve Surgery in Colombo
• Brain Tumors – Expert in safe removal of brain tumors and growths
• Stroke, Brain Bleeds & Aneurysms – Surgery for blocked or burst blood vessels
in the brain
• Spine Problems – Treatment for slipped discs, neck pain, back pain, spine
injuries, and spinal tumors
• Head Injuries & Spine Injuries – Emergency care for accidents, falls, and
trauma
• Nerve Problems – Treatment for nerve pain, nerve compression, nerve injuries,
and nerve tumors in hands, legs, and body
• Migraine & Severe Headaches - Effective treatments for chronic headaches and
migraines
• Children’s Brain & Spine Conditions

Qualifications & Training

• Master in Surgery (MS) - University of Colombo
• Advanced Specialist Training in Brain and Spine Surgery - National Hospital of
Sri Lanka (NHSL) and University Hospital of North Staffordshire, UK
