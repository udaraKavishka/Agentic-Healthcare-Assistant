### Emergency Preparation Guide

Before arriving or while waiting for emergency assistance, follow these basic steps to help our medical team provide faster and safer treatment.

#### 1. Stay Calm and Call for Help

In a life-threatening emergency, call our Emergency Hotline - 0779 511 929 . Stay calm and clearly describe the situation, patient’s condition, and your location. Follow the guidance provided by our emergency response team until help arrives.

#### 2. Before You Arrive

Bring a valid ID (NIC or passport) if possible.
Carry any available medical reports, test results, or prescriptions.
Inform our team about known allergies, chronic illnesses, or ongoing medications.
Let us know if the patient has a pacemaker or medical implant.

#### 3. On Arrival at the Emergency Unit

Our triage nurse will assess the patient’s condition immediately and prioritize treatment based on medical urgency. Please provide accurate details about the patient’s symptoms, history, and medications to assist in diagnosis and treatment.

#### 4. During Treatment

A family member may assist with registration and billing while treatment is ongoing.
Keep personal belongings safe or hand them to a trusted relative.
Our medical team will provide updates on test results and treatment progress.

#### 5. Family Communication

Family members can wait in the Emergency Waiting Area while the patient receives treatment. Updates will be shared by our Emergency Medical Officer or Nursing Team. For safety reasons, please avoid entering restricted areas unless authorized by staff.

#### 6. After Emergency Treatment

Once stabilized, the doctor will decide the next steps - discharge with treatment instructions, admission for observation, or referral for specialized treatment. Please collect all medical documents and prescriptions before leaving.
