Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) 

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 
1. How do I make a hospital reservation?

You can make a hospital reservation by submitting the online booking request with your preferred room category, admission date, and contact information. Our admissions team will contact you to confirm room availability. 

### 
2. Does submitting a reservation guarantee room availability?

No. Your reservation request will be reviewed by our admissions team, and room availability will be confirmed before your booking is finalized. 

### 
3. What room categories are available?

Nawaloka Hospital offers VVIP, VIP Premium, VIP Deluxe, Premium, Deluxe Superior, Deluxe, Executive, and Ward accommodation to meet different patient requirements. 

### 
4. Can I request a specific hospital room?

Yes. During your hospital reservation, you may indicate your preferred room category. Room allocation depends on availability at the time of admission. 

### 
5. Can I make a hospital appointment booking online?

Yes. Patients can submit enquiries for hospital appointment booking and admission assistance through our online booking system. Our admissions team will guide you through the process. 

### 
6. What information is required for room booking?

You will typically need to provide the patient's name, contact number, email address, preferred room category, expected admission date, estimated length of stay, and any additional requirements. 

### 
7. Are private hospital rooms available?

Yes. Nawaloka Hospital provides several private room options, including VVIP, VIP Premium, VIP Deluxe, Premium, Deluxe Superior, Deluxe, and Executive rooms. 

### 
8. Can family members stay with the patient?

Certain room categories include facilities for an accompanying family member, depending on the selected accommodation and hospital policies. 

### 
9. Who should I contact regarding my reservation?

Our admissions team can assist with room availability, admission guidance, booking updates, and any questions related to your hospital stay. 

### 
10. Why choose Nawaloka Hospital for hospital reservation?

Nawaloka Hospital combines modern patient accommodation, experienced healthcare professionals, efficient admission services, and compassionate care to provide a smooth hospital reservation experience and comfortable patient stay in Colombo.
