## Profile

Professional background and areas of expertise

Dr. Prakash Priyadarshan is a highly esteemed consultant cardiologist, renowned for his expertise and dedication to heart health. At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo, Sri Lanka’s No. 1 private hospital, Dr. Priyadarshan delivers world-class cardiac care with a patient-centered approach, earning the trust of patients across Sri Lanka.
His commitment to precision and compassion makes him a top choice for those seeking advanced cardiology solutions.

Expertise in Heart Care You Can Trust
As a consultant cardiologist, Dr. Prakash Priyadarshan specializes in:
• Management of heart failure and cardiomyopathy
• Diagnosis and treatment of coronary artery disease
• Arrhythmia management
• Hypertension and lipid disorders
• Preventive cardiology and risk assessment
• Post-procedure cardiac rehabilitation
Dr. Priyadarshan’s personalized approach ensures every patient receives tailored care for optimal heart health.

Cardiology Services at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo
Nawaloka Hospitals is a leader in cardiology, offering advanced services under specialists like Dr. Priyadarshan. Key services include:
• Advanced cardiac imaging (2D Echo, Cardiac CT, MRI)
• Coronary Angiogram and Angioplasty
• 24-hour Holter Monitoring and stress testing
• Comprehensive cardiac rehabilitation programs
• 24/7 emergency cardiac care
Patients benefit from Nawaloka’s cutting-edge facilities and multidisciplinary care.

Why Choose Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo – Best Hospital for Cardiology?
Patients trust Nawaloka Hospitals and Dr. Prakash Priyadarshan for:
• Exceptional expertise in cardiology
• Access to Sri Lanka’s leading cardiologists
• State-of-the-art diagnostic and treatment facilities
• Compassionate, individualized care
• Convenient central Colombo location
