POLICY ON REMUNERATION 
NAWALOKA HOSPITALS PLC 
This policy is drafted as per the Colombo Stock Exc hange Corporate Governance Rule 9.2.1 (d) and 
9.12. 
The Board Remuneration Committee shall recommend th e Directors' Remuneration Policy to the 
Board. This policy will ensure that remuneration fo r both Executive and Non-Executive Directors is 
handled in a fair, transparent and non-discriminato ry manner, while adhering to the Company’s 
Articles of Association and statutory provisions. 
Key elements of the policy include: 
1. Executive Directors' Remuneration : 
The Committee shall establish and maintain a formal procedure for developing the remuneration 
policy for Executive Directors. The Board shall approve the remuneration packages for Executive 
Directors, ensuring that no Director is involved in determining their own remuneration, in line 
with statutory provisions. 
2. Non-Executive Directors' Remuneration : 
The remuneration of Non-Executive Directors will be based on principles that uphold 
independence and non-discriminatory pay practices. The policy ensures that remuneration does 
not impair the independence of Non-Executive Directors. 
3. Remuneration Approval: 
The Board may approve remuneration or other benefit s for Directors for their services as a 
Director or in any other capacity, subject to the provisions of the Companies Act. The Board also 
has the authority to approve compensation for loss of office or direct any such payments or 
benefits to be authorized by shareholders through an Ordinary Resolution. 
4. Additional Remuneration : 
Any Director who performs services beyond their ord inary duties may be awarded extra 
remuneration, which may be determined by the Board in the form of salary, percentage of profits, 
or other benefits, as deemed appropriate. 
5. Expenses and Allowances : 
The Company may reimburse Directors for reasonable expenses incurred in attending Board, 
Committee or General Meetings, or while conducting business on behalf of the Company. The 
Board may also approve appropriate allowances in respect of such expenses.
