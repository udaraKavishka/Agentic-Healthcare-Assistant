# Vaccination Centre

Safe & Reliable Immunization for All Ages

Vaccinations are a cornerstone of preventive healthcare, offering essential protection against a wide range of preventable diseases. At our Vaccination Centre, we provide a comprehensive range of immunisations-from routine childhood vaccines to adult boosters and travel-related vaccinations-tailored to your individual needs. 

## Here for You Every Day, Every Step

Welcome to Nawaloka Vaccination Centre, open daily from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM for your convenience and comfort. Our caring and skilled team provides trusted, internationally recommended vaccines using the safest techniques for all ages - from infants to seniors. 
We don’t just administer vaccines; we protect what matters most: you and your family. Offering a broad range of essential immunisations-from childhood schedules to travel and adult boosters—our goal is to make preventive treatment simple, accessible, and affordable without compromising quality. 
At Nawaloka, your health is our mission. 

## Complete Vaccination Solutions - Now Closer to You

We offer a full spectrum of WHO & Epid recommended vaccines for all age groups. 

✅ Pre-Pregnancy & Maternal Vaccines - Designed to protect mothers and newborns—before, during, and after pregnancy. 
✅ Baby & Child Immunization Programs - Complete protection following national and international childhood immunization schedules. 
✅ Travel Vaccinations - Essential vaccines tailored to your travel destination and itinerary—stay protected wherever you go. 
✅ University & Student Vaccines - Vaccinations required for students enrolling in foreign universities and higher education institutes abroad. 
✅ Senior Health Immunization - Age-appropriate vaccines to boost immunity and reduce the risk of severe illness in older adults. 
✅ Workplace & Occupational Vaccines - Custom packages for professionals in healthcare, food handling, education, aviation, and more. 
✅ Specialized High-Risk Group Vaccines - For individuals in roles with increased exposure risk—pilots, factory workers, corporate employees, and service industry personnel.
