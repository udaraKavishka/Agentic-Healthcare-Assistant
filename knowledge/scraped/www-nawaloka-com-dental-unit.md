Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) 

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 
1. How often should I visit a Dental Clinic in Colombo?

It is generally recommended to visit a dental clinic Colombo every six months for routine dental examinations and professional cleaning. Regular visits help detect dental problems early and maintain good oral health. 

### 
2. When should I see the best dentist in Colombo?

You should consult the best dentist in Colombo if you experience tooth pain, swollen gums, bleeding gums, sensitivity, loose teeth, dental injuries, or persistent bad breath. Early treatment helps prevent more serious oral health problems. 

### 
3. What services are available at Nawaloka Dental Unit?

Our Dental Unit provides preventive dentistry, restorative dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, root canal treatment, dental implants, gum disease treatment, oral examinations, professional cleaning, and emergency dental services. 

### 
4. What is root canal treatment?

Root canal treatment removes infected tissue from inside the tooth while preserving the natural tooth structure. This procedure helps relieve pain, eliminate infection, and restore normal tooth function. 

### 
5. Are dental implants permanent?

Dental implants are designed as a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth. With proper oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, implants can provide durable and reliable results. 

### 
6. Is dental treatment painful?

Modern dental techniques, advanced equipment, and local anesthesia help ensure that most dental procedures are performed comfortably with minimal discomfort. 

### 
7. Can cosmetic dentistry improve my smile?

Yes. Cosmetic dentistry includes treatments such as teeth whitening, veneers, smile makeovers, and aesthetic restorations that improve the appearance of your teeth while maintaining oral health. 

### 
8. What should I do during a dental emergency?

If you experience severe tooth pain, swelling, bleeding, or dental trauma, contact our Dental Unit immediately for prompt assessment and treatment to prevent further complications. 

### 
9. How do I maintain good oral health?

Maintain good oral health by brushing twice daily, flossing regularly, limiting sugary foods, following a balanced diet, and attending routine dental check-ups for professional cleaning and early detection of dental problems. 

### 
10. Why choose Nawaloka Dental Unit?

Nawaloka Dental Unit combines experienced dentists, advanced technology, comprehensive dental services, and personalized treatment plans to provide high-quality oral healthcare. Patients choose our dental clinic Colombo for professional services, modern facilities, and a commitment to healthy, confident smiles.
