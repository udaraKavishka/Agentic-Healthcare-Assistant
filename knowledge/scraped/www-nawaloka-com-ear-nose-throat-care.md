Frequently Asked Questions 

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 
1. When should I consult the best ENT surgeon in Colombo?

You should consult an ENT specialist if you experience persistent ear pain, hearing loss, chronic sinus problems, nasal blockage, frequent throat infections, voice changes, swallowing difficulties, or dizziness. Early diagnosis helps prevent complications and improves treatment outcomes. 

### 
2. What conditions are treated by an ENT doctor in Sri Lanka?

An ENT doctor in Sri Lanka treats conditions affecting the ears, nose, throat, head, and neck, including hearing loss, sinus infections, allergies, nasal obstruction, tonsillitis, voice disorders, vertigo, sleep apnea, and pediatric ENT conditions. 

### 
3. What ENT surgeries are available at Nawaloka ENT Centre?

Our specialists perform advanced ENT procedures, including endoscopic sinus surgery, septoplasty, tonsillectomy, adenoidectomy, tympanoplasty, mastoidectomy, vocal cord surgery, and other specialized ear, nose, and throat surgeries based on individual patient needs. 

### 
4. Do I need surgery for chronic sinusitis?

Not always. Many patients respond well to medication and lifestyle management. However, if symptoms persist despite treatment, your ENT specialist may recommend minimally invasive endoscopic sinus surgery. 

### 
5. How is hearing loss diagnosed?

Our specialists perform comprehensive hearing evaluations using audiometry, tympanometry, and other advanced diagnostic tests to determine the cause and severity of hearing loss before recommending appropriate treatment. 

### 
6. Can children receive treatment at the ENT Centre?

Yes. Our pediatric ENT specialists provide diagnosis and treatment for children with recurrent ear infections, enlarged tonsils, adenoid problems, hearing loss, allergies, and sleep-related breathing disorders. 

### 
7. What is endoscopic sinus surgery?

Endoscopic sinus surgery is a minimally invasive procedure used to treat chronic sinusitis, nasal polyps, and blocked sinuses. It helps restore normal drainage while minimizing recovery time. 

### 
8. How long does recovery take after ENT surgery?

Recovery depends on the type of ENT procedure performed. Many patients recover within a few days to two weeks, while more complex surgeries may require additional healing time. Your surgeon will provide personalized post-operative instructions. 

### 
9. Do I need a referral to see an ENT specialist?

No. You can directly schedule an appointment with one of our experienced ENT specialists for consultation, diagnosis, and treatment. 

### 
10. Why choose Nawaloka ENT Centre?

Nawaloka ENT Centre combines experienced ENT surgeons in Colombo, advanced diagnostic technology, modern surgical facilities, and personalized treatment plans to provide comprehensive ear, nose, and throat treatment for patients of all ages.
