## Profile

Professional background and areas of expertise

Dr. Usha Samarasinghe is a highly skilled consultant radiologist, celebrated for her expertise in diagnostic imaging and commitment to accurate diagnoses. At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo, Sri Lanka’s premier private hospital, Dr. Samarasinghe provides world-class radiology services, ensuring precision and patient comfort.
Her dedication to excellence makes her a trusted choice for reliable diagnostic solutions.

Expertise in Radiology You Can Trust
Dr. Usha Samarasinghe specializes in:
• Advanced MRI, CT, and ultrasound imaging
• Fluoroscopy and X-ray diagnostics
• Interventional radiology procedures
• Pediatric and adult imaging services
• Comprehensive imaging reports and consultations
Her meticulous approach ensures accurate diagnoses that guide effective treatment plans.

Radiology Services at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo
Nawaloka Hospitals is a leader in diagnostic imaging, offering state-of-the-art services under specialists like Dr. Samarasinghe. Key services include:
• High-resolution MRI and CT scans
• Advanced ultrasound and Doppler studies
• Digital mammography and breast cancer screening
• Interventional radiology for minimally invasive procedures
• 24/7 emergency imaging services
• Comprehensive radiology consultations
Nawaloka’s modern imaging facilities ensure precise and timely diagnostics.
