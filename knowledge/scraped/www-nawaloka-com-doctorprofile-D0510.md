## Profile

Professional background and areas of expertise

Prof. Arjuna De Silva is a distinguished physician and academic, renowned for his expertise in internal medicine and commitment to patient care. At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo, Sri Lanka’s No. 1 private hospital, Prof. De Silva delivers world-class care with a focus on precision and compassion.
His leadership in the medical field makes him a trusted choice for comprehensive health solutions.

Expertise in Internal Medicine You Can Trust
Prof. Arjuna De Silva specializes in:
• Management of chronic conditions like diabetes and hypertension
• Diagnosis and treatment of infectious diseases
• Respiratory and cardiovascular conditions
• Preventive health screenings and check-ups
• Lifestyle and nutritional counseling
• Acute illness management
His thorough approach ensures patients receive personalized treatment plans for optimal health.

Internal Medicine Services at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo
Nawaloka Hospitals is a leader in internal medicine, offering advanced services under specialists like Prof. De Silva. Key services include:
• Comprehensive health assessments and diagnostics
• Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, ultrasound)
• Chronic disease management programs
• 24/7 emergency medical services
• Multidisciplinary care for complex conditions
• In-patient and out-patient care
Nawaloka’s modern facilities ensure precise diagnostics and effective treatments.
