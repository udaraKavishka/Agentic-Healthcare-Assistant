## IT Support Desk - School Leaver

Colombo 02 Full Time School Leaver / Entry Level

Posted
18/08/2026

Closing Date
18/09/2026

About the position
Nawaloka Hospitals PLC is seeking a motivated and responsible school leaver to join the IT Support Desk team. The selected candidate will assist users with basic technical issues and support the smooth operation of hospital IT systems.

## Key Responsibilities

Receive and record IT support requests from hospital staff.

Provide first-level assistance for basic hardware and software issues.

Assist users with login, password, printer, email, and system-access problems.

Install and configure computers, printers, and other IT equipment under supervision.

Escalate complex technical issues to relevant IT team members.

Update the IT helpdesk system with issue details and resolution status.

Support IT equipment and asset records.

Assist with basic network and connectivity troubleshooting.

Follow IT security, confidentiality, and data-protection procedures.

Provide courteous and professional support to all users.

## Requirements

Basic knowledge of computers, operating systems, and software applications.

Familiarity with Microsoft Windows and Microsoft Office applications.

Basic understanding of computer hardware, printers, and networking.

Ability to identify and troubleshoot basic IT-related issues.

Good verbal and written communication skills.

Strong customer service and interpersonal skills.

Willingness to learn new systems and technologies.

Ability to work responsibly in a fast-paced environment.

## Qualifications

G.C.E. Advanced Level qualification, preferably with ICT as a subject.

Fresh school leavers are encouraged to apply.

Previous IT-related training or certification will be an added advantage.

## Skills & Competencies

IT Support Windows MS Office Hardware Networking Troubleshooting Customer Service Data Security

## What We Offer

Structured on-the-job learning

Exposure to hospital IT systems

Professional development opportunities

Collaborative IT environment
