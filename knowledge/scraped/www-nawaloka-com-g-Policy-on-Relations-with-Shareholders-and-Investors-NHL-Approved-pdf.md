POLICY ON RELATIONS WITH SHAREHOLDERS AND INVESTORS 
NAWALOKA HOSPITALS PLC 
 
This policy is drafted as per the Colombo Stock Exchange Corporate Governance rule 9.2.1 (g). 
 
1. Introduction to the Policy 
 
1.1 General 
This Policy outlines the Company's approach to maintaining transparent and effective communication 
with its shareholders and investors. The Company is committed to fair disclosure practices and strives 
to provide up -to-date information via electronic communication whenever possible, subject to 
statutory regulations. 
 
1.2 Board Approval and Review 
The Board of Directors is responsible for fostering ongoing communication with shareholders and 
reviewing this policy regularly to ensure its effectiveness. 
 
2. Approval of Communications with Shareholders 
All communications with shareholders will be approved by the Board. If this is not feasible, the 
Chairman, Deputy Chairman and/or Deputy General Manager will approve communications. 
 
3. General Meetings 
 
3.1 Meetings 
The Annual General Meeting (AGM) and other general meetings are the primary forums for company-
shareholder communication. Shareholders are encouraged to actively participate. 
 
3.2 Notices of General Meetings 
Notices and related documents for the AGM will be sent as per statutory requirements. Shareholders 
are encouraged to attend in person or appoint proxies to vote on their behalf. 
 
3.3 Suggestions/Comments 
The Company will make every effort to consider and implement shareholders’ suggestions from 
general meetings. 
 
4. Communication Policy 
 
4.1 Provision of Written Information 
The Annual Report is the primary communication tool. It will be: 
 Informative: Containing relevant financial, operational and community -impacting 
information. 
 Available: Distributed promptly to all shareholders and stakeholders. 
 
4.2 Electronic Communication 
 
The Company aims to move toward electronic communication, encouraging shareholders to opt for 
email updates, while still providing paper-based communications if preferred. The website will serve 
as a key platform for shareholder-related information. 
 
 Communication via E -mail: Shareholders are responsible for providing and updating their 
email addresses.

 Communication via Post : The Company will continue to use postal communication where 
shareholders have not opted for electronic communication. 
 
5. Access to Directors, Management and Auditors 
 
Shareholders can contact the Directors via the Company’s website or written correspondence to the 
Company Secretary. 
 
During general meetings, shareholders can ask questions, including inquiries related to audit matters, 
which will be addressed by the auditors. 
 
6. Investing/Divesting Decisions 
 
Shareholders are expected to exercise due care and/or seek independent advice before 
making investment or divestment decisions. 
 
7. Reporting of Shareholder Concerns 
 
All concerns raised by shareholders will be reported to the Board at the subsequent meeting. 
 
8. Access to the Policy 
 
This policy will be made available on the Company's website.
