## Sri Lanka’s First Private Bone Marrow Transplant Centre

Nawaloka Hospitals established the country’s first dedicated Bone Marrow Transplant Unit in 2014, offering advanced hematopoietic stem cell transplantation for both adults and children. With more than 200 successful transplants and an 85% survival rate, we are recognized as a trusted centre for bone marrow transplant in Sri Lanka. 

### Our Transplant Services

We provide both Autologous (patient’s own cells) and Allogeneic (donor cells) bone marrow transplants. Patients from Sri Lanka and abroad seek our expertise in treating: 

• Multiple Myeloma 
• Non-Hodgkin and Hodgkin Lymphoma 
• Acute Leukaemias (AML, ALL) 
• Thalassaemia Major 
• Aplastic Anemia 
• Neuroblastoma 
• Bone Marrow Failure Syndrome 
• Multiple Sclerosis 
Our Bone Marrow Transplant Unit in Colombo is regarded as one of the best hospitals for stem cell transplant in Sri Lanka, offering advanced therapies tailored to each condition. 

### Global Diagnostics & Support Services

To ensure precision and safety, we collaborate with internationally accredited laboratories and donor registries: 

• High-resolution HLA Typing (USA laboratory) 
• Chimerism Testing for post-transplant monitoring (USA) 
• Access to worldwide donor registries for matching 
This international support makes Nawaloka a reliable choice for patients looking for stem cell donor matching and transplant expertise in Sri Lanka. 

### Patient Treatment and Safety

Our model prioritizes safety, comfort, and long-term outcomes. We provide: 

• Comprehensive pre-transplant evaluation 
• Dedicated post-transplant monitoring 
• 24/7 ICU with infection control 
• A multidisciplinary team of experienced specialists 
Each patient receives an individualized treatment plan to achieve the best possible recovery and long-term survival. 

### Comprehensive Patient Support

Beyond transplantation, we ensure every patient receives complete support: 

• Lifetime post-transplant follow-up including vaccination schedules 
• Cryopreservation of stem cells for future treatments 
• Donor Lymphocyte Infusion (DLI) procedures 
• Guidance with medical documentation, funding, and leave letters 
These services strengthen Nawaloka’s position as a leader in bone marrow transplant treatment in Sri Lanka.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Bone Marrow Transplants and advanced therapies at Nawaloka Hospitals. 

## 
What is a bone marrow transplant?

It is a medical procedure that replaces damaged or diseased bone marrow with healthy stem cells to restore normal blood cell production. 

## 
How long is recovery after a transplant?

Patients usually stay in hospital for 3-4 weeks, followed by regular long-term follow-up. 

## 
Who can be a donor?

Donors may be siblings, family members (haploidentical donors), or unrelated donors from international registries. 

## 
Is bone marrow transplant available in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Nawaloka Hospitals is the first private hospital in Sri Lanka to offer bone marrow transplants, providing world-class service locally. 

## 
What is CAR-T Cell Therapy?

A breakthrough treatment in which a patient’s T-cells are genetically engineered to fight cancers such as leukaemia and lymphoma.
