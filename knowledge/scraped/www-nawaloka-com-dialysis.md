Dialysis Unit

At Nawaloka Hospitals, we understand that Dialysis Treatment is not just a treatment - it’s about giving patients a better quality of life and peace of mind. We bring the same dedication, compassion, and world-class standards to our Dialysis Unit at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo 02.
Why Choose Us?
Our Dialysis Unit is equipped with the latest B. Braun dialysis machines and a high-efficiency Reverse Osmosis (RO) water purification system, ensuring every treatment meets global safety and performance standards.

We offer both inpatient and outpatient dialysis, with flexible scheduling, real-time monitoring, and full consultant supervision.
Comfort. Safety. Precision.

Dedicated Dialysis Bays with privacy-enhancing design.

Continuous Monitoring of vitals: BP, oxygen levels, heart rate.

Emergency-Ready Setup with ICU-trained staff and on-site crash carts.

Strict Infection Control Protocols including isolation zones for high-risk patients.
Now Available: Private VIP Dialysis Room
For those who prefer added comfort and discretion, our VIP Dialysis Room offers:

A private, fully equipped treatment space

Personalized nursing support

Optional guest seating and entertainment

Priority scheduling and enhanced amenities
Ideal for executive clients, international patients, or those seeking a more personalized dialysis experience.
Holistic Kidney Treatment
Your dialysis journey is managed by a multidisciplinary team of consultant nephrologists, specialist nurses, dietitians, and coordinators - ensuring every session is aligned with your health goals.

We’re also proud to support over 500 successful kidney transplants to date through our integrated Renal Transplant Program.
