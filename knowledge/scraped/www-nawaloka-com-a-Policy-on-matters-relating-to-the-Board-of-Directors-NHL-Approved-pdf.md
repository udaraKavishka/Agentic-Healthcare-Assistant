POLICY ON MATTERS RELATING TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS 
NAWALKA HOSPITALS PLC 
 
1. INTRODUCTION 
 
The Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Nawaloka Hospitals PLC (the “Company”) recognizes the 
importance of good corporate governance and corporate social responsibility in promoting and 
strengthening the trust of its shareholders and all other stakeholders and believes proceeding in 
accordance with good corporate governance and corporate social responsibility practices. The Board shall 
conduct itself with the highest standard of ethics and proceed in accordance with the applicable laws and 
regulations, to add value to the shareholders and stakeholders. As per section 9.2.1 (a) & 9.5 of the 
Colombo Stock Exchange Corporate Governance rules the listed entity shall establish and maintain a 
formal policy governing matters relating to the Board of Directors. 
 
2. BALANCE OF REPRESENTATION BETWEEN EXECUTIVE AND NON- EXECUTIVE DIRECTORS 
 
The Board recognizes the need for a balance of representation between Executive and Non -Executive 
Directors and cover at minimum board composition, the roles and functions of the Chairperson and Chief 
Executive Officer or equiva lent position (hereinafter commonly referred to as the CEO), Board balance 
and procedures for the appraisal of Board performance and the appraisal of the CEO. 
 
The role of the Chairperson and CEO have been combined, 
(i) the rationale for combining such po sitions is that Dr. H. K. Jayantha Dharmadasa, Chairman / Chief 
Executive Officer of the Company has exceptional knowledge and expertise in relation to the operations 
of the Company, being its Chief Executive Officer since inception and being its Chairman consequent to 
the demise of its Founder Chairman and also as he is the main shareholder of the Company considering 
his direct and indirect shareholding; and, 
(ii) the Board Charter contains terms of reference/functions of the Senior Independent Director (SID) and 
the powers of the SID, which are equivalent to that of the Chairperson in the instance of a conflict of 
interest. 
(iii) measures, including complete independence for the SID to act, have been implemented to safeguard 
the interests of the SID. 
 
3. BOARD DIVERSITY 
 
There shall be diversity in Board composition for Board effectiveness in terms of a range of experience, 
skills, competencies, age, gender, industry requirements and importance of objective selection of 
directors. 
 
4. BOARD COMPOSITION 
 
The number of Directors on the Board shall be not more than 12, subject to the statutes. This is required 
to have directors with expertise on the numerous areas required to steer the Company effectively and in 
a profitable manner. Directors are appointed by t he board as and when casual vacancies arise, with the 
recommendation of the Board Nominations and Governance Committee, and with the relevant statutory 
approvals.

5. FREQUENCY OF BOARD MEETINGS 
 
The Board shall meet regularly and board meetings shall be held at least eleven times a year at 
approximately monthly intervals. Such regular board meetings shall normally involve active participation 
in person of a majority of directors entitled to be pre sent. Obtaining the board’s consent through the 
circulation of written resolutions/papers shall be avoided as far as possible. 
 
6. COMPLIANCE WITH LISTING RULES 
 
A mechanism shall be provided, through the Company Secretary, for ensuring that Directors ar e kept 
abreast of the Listing Rules and on -going compliance and/or non -compliance by the Listed Entity with 
obligations arising under such Rules. 
 
7. ATTENDANCE AT BOARD MEETINGS 
 
A Director must attend a minimum of 6 meetings (more than 50%) held during the calendar year in order 
to ensure consistent attendance at Board Meetings and to avoid being deemed to vacate such position. 
 
8. REQUIREMENTS ON TRADING IN SECURITIES OF THE LISTED ENTITY AND ITS LISTED GROUP 
 
Directors shall immediately inform the Company Secretary if they trade in securities of the Listed Entity. 
Directors shall not trade in Listed Entity’s securities within the stipulated time frames, as per section 8.6 
which is given below. 
 
Section 8.6 - Trading by connected parties 
Connected persons should not trade, borrow or lend on the basis of price sensitive information that has 
not been disclosed to the public. Moreover, connected persons shall not trade, borrow or lend the 
Securities of the Listed Entity even after release of the informa tion to the Exchange for a period, which 
should not be less than two (02) Market Days after the release of the information to permit thorough 
public dissemination and evaluation thereof. In computing this period of two (02) Market Days, the day 
on which di sclosure is made will be excluded. For the purposes of this Rule, an individual is connected 
with an Entity if, and only if:- 
a. he is a director of that Entity or a related Entity or his spouse and children under 18 years of age; or 
b. he occupies a position as an officer (other than director) or employee of that Entity or a related Entity; 
or 
c. a position involving a professional business relationship between himself (or his employer or an Entity 
of which he is a director) and the first Entity or a rel ated Entity which in either case may reasonably be 
expected to give him access to information which, in relation to Listed Securities of either Entity, is 
unpublished price sensitive information and which it would be reasonable to expect (a person in his 
position not to disclose except) for the proper performance of his function. 
 
9. MAXIMUM NUMBER OF DIRECTORSHIPS IN LISTED COMPANIES 
 
A Director of the Company shall not hold office as a director of more than 20 Listed Entities.

10. PARTICIPATION AT BOARD MEETINGS 
 
All Directors shall have the right to participate at meetings of the Board and Board Committees by audio 
visual means and such participation shall be taken into account when deciding on the quorum.
