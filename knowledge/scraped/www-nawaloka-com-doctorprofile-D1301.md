## Profile

Professional background and areas of expertise 

Dr. Maiya Gunasekera is a highly respected surgeon, known for his precision and dedication to patient safety. 
At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo, Sri Lanka’s premier private hospital, Dr. Gunasekera delivers world-class surgical care in a state of the art environment.
His expertise makes him a trusted choice for those seeking advanced surgical solutions.
