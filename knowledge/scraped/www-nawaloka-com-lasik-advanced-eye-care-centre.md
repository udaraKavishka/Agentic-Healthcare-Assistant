## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 
1. When should I visit an eye doctor Colombo?

You should consult an eye doctor Colombo if you experience blurred vision, eye pain, redness, headaches, double vision, sensitivity to light, or any sudden changes in your eyesight. Regular eye examinations are also recommended to maintain good eye health. 

### 
2. What eye conditions are treated at the Nawaloka Eye Treatment Centre?

Our specialists diagnose and treat a wide range of eye conditions, including cataracts, glaucoma, refractive errors, retinal disorders, dry eye syndrome, eye infections, and age-related vision problems. 

### 
3. What types of eye surgery are available?

The Eye Treatment Centre offers advanced eye surgery procedures, including cataract surgery, glaucoma procedures, retinal surgery, corneal treatments, refractive surgery, and other specialized ophthalmic treatments based on individual patient needs. 

### 
4. What is LASIK treatment, and am I eligible for it?

LASIK is a vision correction procedure designed to reduce dependence on glasses or contact lenses. Eligibility depends on factors such as age, eye health, corneal thickness, and prescription stability. A comprehensive eye assessment is required to determine suitability. 

### 
5. How often should I have an eye examination?

Adults should generally have an eye examination every one to two years. Individuals with diabetes, glaucoma, high-risk factors, or existing eye conditions may require more frequent check-ups. 

### 
6. Do I need a referral to book an appointment with an eye specialist?

No. Patients can directly book an appointment with our eye specialists for consultations, diagnostic investigations, and treatment services. 

### 
7. What are refractive services?

Refractive services focus on diagnosing and correcting vision problems such as short-sightedness (myopia), long-sightedness (hyperopia), astigmatism, and presbyopia through advanced assessments and treatment options. 

### 
8. How long does recovery take after eye surgery?

Recovery time varies depending on the type of procedure performed. Many patients experience improved vision within a few days, while complete healing may take several weeks. Your specialist will provide personalized post-operative guidance. 

### 
9. Are eye investigations painful?

Most eye investigations are non-invasive and painless. Diagnostic tests help specialists accurately assess eye health, detect conditions early, and recommend the most suitable treatment plan. 

### 
10. Why choose Nawaloka Eye Treatment Centre for eye treatment and eye surgery?

Nawaloka Eye Treatment Centre combines experienced ophthalmologists, advanced technology, comprehensive diagnostic services, and modern surgical facilities to provide high-quality vision treatment in Colombo.
