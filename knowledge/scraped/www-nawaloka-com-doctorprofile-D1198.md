## Profile

Professional background and areas of expertise

Dr. M.T.D. Lakshan is a highly skilled consultant ENT surgeon, known for his expertise in treating ear, nose, and throat conditions with precision and care. At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo, Sri Lanka’s premier private hospital, Dr. Lakshan delivers world-class ENT care in a patient-centered environment. He leverages advanced IT systems and telemedicine solutions to provide remote consultations, follow-ups, and digital health monitoring, ensuring patients receive timely and convenient care. His dedication to patient comfort makes him a trusted choice for ENT solutions.

Expertise in ENT Care You Can Trust
Dr. M.T.D. Lakshan specializes in:
• Treatment of sinusitis and nasal disorders
• Management of hearing loss and ear infections
• Throat and voice disorder treatments
• Minimally invasive ENT surgeries
• Pediatric ENT care
• Allergy and sleep apnea management
His meticulous approach ensures optimal outcomes and patient satisfaction.

ENT Services at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo
Nawaloka Hospitals is a leader in ENT care, offering advanced services under specialists like Dr. Lakshan. Key services include:
• Endoscopic sinus surgeries
• Advanced audiology and hearing assessments
• Tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy
• Micro-laryngeal surgeries for voice disorders
• 24/7 emergency ENT care
• Comprehensive ENT diagnostics
Nawaloka’s modern facilities ensure the highest standards of care and safety.

Why Choose Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo – Best Hospital for ENT Care?
Patients trust Nawaloka Hospitals and Dr. M.T.D. Lakshan for:
• Exceptional expertise in ENT surgery
• Access to Sri Lanka’s top ENT specialists
• State-of-the-art diagnostic and surgical facilities
• Compassionate, patient-centered care
• Convenient central Colombo location
