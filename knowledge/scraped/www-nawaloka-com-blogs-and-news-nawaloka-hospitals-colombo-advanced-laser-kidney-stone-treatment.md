## Nawaloka Hospitals PLC Colombo Sets New Standards in Kidney Stone Treatment with Advanced Laser Technology

Nawaloka Hospitals PLC Colombo has introduced a state-of-the-art laser-assisted ureteroscopic (URS) stone treatment, reinforcing its position as a leader in advanced kidney stone management in Sri Lanka. This latest addition to its urological services represents a major advancement in patient treatment, offering a highly precise, minimally invasive solution that eliminates the need for surgical incisions or stitches, leaves no visible scarring, and enables faster recovery with shorter hospital stays. Nawaloka Hospitals PLC Colombo continues its tradition in being the pioneer in introducing the latest medical technology. 
By combining advanced laser technology with real-time imaging, the procedure offers exceptional precision, enhanced patient comfort, and excellent clinical outcomes, enabling patients to recover faster and return to their daily lives sooner. 
Unlike traditional open surgery, which often required large incisions, prolonged hospitalization, and lengthy recovery periods, Nawaloka’s advanced laser-assisted URS treatment allows kidney and ureteric stones to be treated through the body’s natural urinary passage. Using an ultra-thin flexible scope equipped with a miniature camera, specialists can accurately locate stones even in hard-to-reach areas of the urinary tract. A high-precision laser is then used to break the stones into tiny fragments, which are either removed during the procedure or naturally passed through the urinary system. This minimally invasive approach eliminates the need for major surgery while delivering safer, more effective, and patient-friendly treatment outcomes. 
A key advantage of this advanced treatment is its convenience for patients. Most procedures are performed as day cases, allowing patients to undergo treatment and return home on the same day following medical assessment and recovery. 
Every patient undergoes a comprehensive diagnostic evaluation prior to treatment, including advanced imaging studies to assess the stone’s size, location, and complexity. Multidisciplinary urology team at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo then develops a personalized treatment plan aligned with internationally accepted clinical standards to ensure the best possible outcomes. 
As one of Sri Lanka’s most established and respected private medical service provider, Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo continues to invest in world-class medical technology and specialist expertise. Patients benefit from access to advanced minimally invasive laser systems with most experienced and qualified urology specialists in SriLanka, comprehensive diagnostic and surgical services, and dedicated post-treatment monitoring and recovery support. 
The seamless integration of cutting-edge technology, expert clinical team, and patient-centered services has firmly positioned Nawaloka Hospitals PLC Colombo as a trusted destination for advanced kidney stone treatment in Sri Lanka. 
With the introduction of laser-assisted flexible URS, kidney stone management at Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo is entering a new era defined by precision, safety, convenience, and faster recovery. Through this pioneering advancement, Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo continues to bring world-class urological care closer to Sri Lankan patients, reducing the need for traditional surgery while delivering superior treatment outcomes and an enhanced patient experience.
