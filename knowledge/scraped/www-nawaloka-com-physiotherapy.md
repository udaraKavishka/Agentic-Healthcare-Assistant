Our Core Physiotherapy Services ✅ Pain Management (Joint & Muscle)

Say goodbye to chronic pain. Our joint and muscle physiotherapy specialists offer targeted therapy to reduce inflammation, increase flexibility, and eliminate discomfort — restoring your freedom of movement. ✅ Neurological Rehabilitation

Regain independence with specialized rehab programs for stroke, spinal injuries, Parkinson’s disease, and more. Improve balance, coordination, and strength under expert neurological guidance. ✅ Arthritis Rehabilitation

Find relief from osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis through gentle, effective physiotherapy. Reduce stiffness and pain while improving joint mobility and daily functioning. ✅ Orthopaedic Pre/Post-Operative Wellness

Optimize surgical outcomes with pre- and post-operative orthopaedic physiotherapy. From joint replacements to spinal surgery, our therapies support quicker healing and stronger recovery. ✅ Musculoskeletal Therapy

Our musculoskeletal physiotherapy addresses back pain, neck pain, postural issues, and repetitive strain injuries with evidence-based, functional movement therapy. ✅ Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Get back in the game with advanced wellness for sprains, ligament tears, overuse injuries, and fractures. Our sports therapists help athletes recover faster and reduce risk of re-injury. ✅ Pediatric Physiotherapy

Supporting children with cerebral palsy, Down syndrome, and developmental delays, our child-friendly therapy sessions focus on improving motor skills and mobility in a safe environment. ✅ Geriatric Physiotherapy

Empowering seniors to stay active and independent. We address balance disorders, arthritis, falls, and post-hospital rehab for elderly patients with compassionate, specialized treatment.

## Specialized Therapies at Nawaloka Physiotherapy Unit

Chest Physiotherapy for Inward and OPD Patients: Effective treatments for respiratory issues, including chronic bronchitis and pneumonia.
COVID-19 Recovery Physiotherapy: Customized rehab for post-COVID recovery to rebuild strength and lung capacity.
Pulmonary Function Testing: Accurate diagnosis and monitoring of respiratory performance for medical or surgical purposes.
Inhalation Therapy: For improved breathing and airway clearance in both outpatients and inpatients.
Shock Wave Therapy: Advanced treatment for chronic tendon pain, heel spurs, and musculoskeletal disorders.
