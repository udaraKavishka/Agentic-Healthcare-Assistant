POLICY ON RISK MANAGEMENT AND INTERNAL CONTROLS
NAWALOKA HOSPITALS PLC
1. DEFINITIONS
 ‘Board’: Board of Directors of C T Land Development PLC
 ‘Company’: C T Land Development PLC
 ‘CSE’: Colombo Stock Exchange
 ‘Listing Rules’: Listing Rules of the Colombo Stock Exchange
2. PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVE
The Risk Management and Internal Controls Policy establishes the framework and procedures
adopted by C T Land Development PLC to effectively manage risks and maintain robust internal
controls. The policy aims to safeguard company assets, ensure the reliability of financial reporting, and
ensure compliance with relevant laws, regulations and standards, including those stipulated by the
CSE.
3. SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees, directors, and officers of C T Land Development PLC. It covers the
management of financial, operational, compliance, strategic and reputational risks, with adequate
internal controls in place.
4. RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK
4.1 Risk Identification
Risk assessments will identify principal u ncertainties affecting the Company, including potential
threats to the business model, performance, solvency or liquidity. Financial and non-financial risks will
be identified by the Audit Committee, with input from Management and employees where necessary.
4.2 Assessment and Evaluation
Identified risks are assessed and categorized as high, medium or low based on their likelihood and
impact on the Company. Tolerance levels and trigger points will be set for each risk. The risk
assessments will be reviewed by the Audit Committee and subsequently by the Board.
4.3 Risk Management
Each identified risk will be assigned to a risk owner ( Manager) responsible for reporting to the Audit
Committee. Global trends and risks will also be considered. Mitigation strategies, such as avoidance,
reduction, sharing or acceptance, will be employed depending on the nature of each risk.
4.4 Monitoring
The Company will monitor risks at various organizational levels, including through the Audit
Committee and Board. Regular reviews of the risk registers and performance of mitigation plans will
ensure ongoing oversight.

5. INTERNAL CONTROL FRAMEWORK
5.1 Control Environment
The Company will maintain a strong control environment, emphasizing ethical behavior and
competence. The Board and senior management will foster a culture of integrity aligned with the
values of the Company, while adhering to local laws.
5.2 Control Activities
Policies, procedures and practices will be implemented to mitigate risks and ensure the Company’s
objectives are achieved. These control activities will be embedded into the business operations.
5.3 Information and Communication
The Company will ensure timely co mmunication of relevant risk and internal control information
across all levels. Efficient communication channels will be established for the smooth flow of
information.
5.4 Monitoring of Controls
Internal controls will be regularly monitored through inter nal audits, management reviews and
external audits. Identified deficiencies will be addressed promptly with corrective actions.
6. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
6.1 Board of Directors
The Board is responsible for overseeing the risk management and internal co ntrol frameworks,
ensuring sufficient resources for effective implementation.
6.2 Audit Committee
The Audit Committee will assist the Board in monitoring risk management and internal control
effectiveness by reviewing risk reports, audit findings and other relevant information, and making
recommendations to the Board.
6.3 Management
Management will implement the risk management and internal control frameworks, ensuring that
risks are identified, assessed and mitigated.
6.4 Employees
Employees are responsibl e for adhering to the risk management and internal control policies. They
are encouraged to report any risks or deficiencies through established channels.
7. REVIEW AND UPDATE
The Audit Committee will review this policy annually or as needed to ensure its relevance and
effectiveness. Updates will be approved by the Board.

8. COMPLIANCE AND REPORTING
The Company is committed to complying with all applicable laws and regulations related to risk
management and internal controls. Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary action,
including termination of employment.
